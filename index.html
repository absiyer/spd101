01

Validating an idea

Fall in love with the problem.
Not the idea.

Obsessing over the problem means you can answer these

  • Who exactly is this person?
  • What's their whole day, without you in it?
  • Which minute of it does your product appear in?
  • What are they doing right before, and right after?
  • What do they do today instead?
  • What does that cost them?
  • Who else is in the room? Who can veto it?
  • Which use case is most frequent — not most interesting?
